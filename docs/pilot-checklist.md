# Soldier pilot checklist

For a Soldier trying the translator and resume builder. Fill in one copy per specialty you test.
Your own MOS/AOC and rank are enough to get a publication-based result; you do not have to write anything about your own work.

**Soldier:** ____________  **MOS/AOC:** ______  **Rank:** ______  **Component:** Active / Reserve / Guard  **Date:** ______

## A. Check the result (flag anything wrong)

Mark **OK** or **FLAG**. For every FLAG write what the screen said and what it should say.

| # | Check | OK | FLAG | What it said / what it should say |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Specialty title.** Is the official title shown for your MOS/AOC the one on your orders or record? | ☐ | ☐ | |
| 2 | **Rank duties.** Do the duties listed for your grade describe what a Soldier of your rank in this specialty is expected to do? (Not what you personally did.) | ☐ | ☐ | |
| 3 | **Unsupported skills.** Is any skill, system, number, award, certification or accomplishment on the resume that you did not do or tell the app about? Are any suggested skills on the resume that you did not select? | ☐ | ☐ | |
| 4 | **Missing information.** Is something you expected missing: a duty of your specialty, a grade, a position, a qualification? | ☐ | ☐ | |

Also confirm (each should be YES):

- ☐ Suggested skills were **not** selected until I selected them, and only the ones I selected are under CORE COMPETENCIES.
- ☐ Every line from the publication is marked **[CONFIRM]** and sits under SPECIALTY SCOPE, not under YOUR ACCOMPLISHMENTS.
- ☐ Anything the app did not know is a visible **[ADD: …]** prompt, not invented text.
- ☐ Recommended training, schools and assignments appear only in the career-development panel, never as something I did.

## B. Career-development guidance gaps (record separately)

This is a different list from section A. A gap here is **not** an error in the result. It means the app has no career-development guidance for you. Use it when the career-development panel says guidance is not available, or leaves out something your career guide covers.

| Question | Answer |

| Which grade or section was missing? | |

## How these are tracked

- **A1–A4 flags** are corrections to what the app shows now. They go on a "corrections" list and are checked against the approved source before any change.
- **Section B** answers go on a "guidance coverage" list. They are compared with `data/reference/corpus/guide-coverage-reconciliation.md`, which already lists each specialty without guidance and the reason. A gap there is **not** counted as a correction and does not change which sources are approved.
- Neither list changes a source approval by itself. A source is approved or withdrawn only by the owner, after the source is checked.
